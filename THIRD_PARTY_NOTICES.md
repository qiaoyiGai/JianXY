## Self-hosted fonts

- Noto Sans SC: Google Fonts distribution, subset to the current site's characters, weights 400–600. License: [SIL Open Font License 1.1](public/fonts/NotoSansSC-OFL.txt).
- Manrope: Google Fonts Latin variable font, weights 400–600. License: [SIL Open Font License 1.1](public/fonts/Manrope-OFL.txt).

New Chinese characters not present in the subset fall back to the system font; regenerate the subset when updating portfolio copy.
